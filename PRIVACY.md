# Privacy Policy — Nopilepsy

**Last updated: April 5, 2026**

## Data Collection

Nopilepsy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data with anyone. Period.

## Health Data

Nopilepsy reads health sensor data from Apple HealthKit (sleep, heart rate, HRV, SpO2, temperature, activity, ECG) solely to calculate a seizure risk assessment. This data is processed entirely on your device and never leaves it.

## Storage

All data (risk assessments, baseline models, medication logs, seizure history) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no cloud storage, no remote database, no server.

## Network

Nopilepsy makes zero network requests. The app has no server, no API, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting. The only network activity possible is if you manually tap a research citation link, which opens in Safari — this is your action, not the app's.

## Watch Connectivity

If you use Nopilepsy on both iPhone and Apple Watch, risk data syncs between your paired devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This is a direct device-to-device connection managed by iOS. No data passes through any external server.

## Third Parties

Nopilepsy has zero third-party dependencies. No SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no ad networks, no tracking pixels. The app uses only Apple's first-party frameworks.

## Data Export

You can export your risk history as PDF or CSV. This is entirely user-initiated through the iOS share sheet. Nopilepsy does not automatically share data with anyone.

## Open Source

Nopilepsy is open source under the MIT license. You can audit every line of code at https://github.com/karelkucerovsky-bit/nopilepsy.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/karelkucerovsky-bit/nopilepsy/issues.
