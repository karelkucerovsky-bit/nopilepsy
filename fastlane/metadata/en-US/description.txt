Passive seizure risk monitoring for Apple Watch and iPhone.

Nopilepsy reads your health sensors and shows one number — your current seizure risk level. Tap to see which factors contribute most. All processing happens on your device.

FEATURES
- 9-factor risk engine: sleep, HRV, heart rate, SpO2, temperature, activity, ECG, stress, medication adherence
- Personal baseline learning that adapts to your patterns over 14 days
- Research-backed: every risk factor weight links to published neurology studies
- Medication dose tracking with adherence scoring
- Full risk history with trend visualization
- PDF and CSV export for neurologist appointments

PRIVACY
All health data stays on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No analytics. No tracking. Zero data collection. Open source under MIT license — verify every line yourself at github.com/karelkucerovsky-bit/nopilepsy.

IMPORTANT
Nopilepsy is not a medical device. Risk assessments are informational only, based on published research correlations. Always consult your neurologist for medical decisions.
