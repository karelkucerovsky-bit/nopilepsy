Description
Passive seizure risk monitoring for Apple Watch. Reads your health sensors, shows one number. Tap to understand why.
  • 9-Factor Risk Engine Sleep, HRV, heart rate, SpO2, temperature, activity, ECG, stress, medication
  • Personal Baseline Learns your patterns over 14 days
  • Research Citations Every weight links to published neurology research
  • Medication Tracking Log doses, track adherence timing
  • Export for Your Doctor PDF and CSV with full history
  • On-Device Only No cloud. No accounts. No tracking.
